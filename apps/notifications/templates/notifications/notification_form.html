Writing a good notification

  • Be clear and concise

    A short title and a plain-language message read fastest.

  • Point to something actionable

    Mention the property, tenant, or item involved so the reader knows what to do next.

  • Save it for what matters

    Reserve notifications for things the recipient genuinely needs to know.

  • Check the preview

    Read it back on the left before sending — it can't be recalled once sent.
