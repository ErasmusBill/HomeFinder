Our Story

Why We Built VacantHommie

Finding a decent, affordable, and legit rental property in Ghana has traditionally been frustrating. Prospective tenants often pay high "viewing fees" only to be shown substandard properties or scammed by unregistered brokers.

VacantHommie was born to fix this broken system. We provide an intuitive, verified platform where landlords can showcase their vacancies with official documentation (such as Ghana Card and Title/Site Plans), and tenants can search, inspect, and schedule visits with absolute peace of mind.
