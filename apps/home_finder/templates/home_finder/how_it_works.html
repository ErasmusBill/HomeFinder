Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about using VacantHommie.

Do tenants pay any fee to browse or view properties?

No! Browsing listings, filtering by location/price, and submitting viewing requests or expressing interest is completely free for tenants.

How long does landlord document verification take?

Our administrative team reviews uploaded Ghana Cards and property documents promptly—usually within a few hours to 1 business day.

Can a landlord use one Ghana Card for all listings?

Yes! Once your Ghana Card is uploaded and approved, it acts as a platform-wide identity verification covering all your current and future property listings.

What should I do if I encounter an issue or have a question?

You can reach our dedicated support team via our Contact Page anytime and we will assist you immediately.
